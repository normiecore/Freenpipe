# W2-08 Windows smoke checks

Entries follow the format in [../windows-smoke-checklist.md](../windows-smoke-checklist.md). Run
them on a W2-08 (or later) build in the isolated dev setup: `FP_DD`, port 3035,
`SCREENPIPE_API_KEY`. `api` and `sqlq` are the helpers from PLAN.md section 4. The in-process
route tests (`cargo test -p screenpipe-engine --test meetings_v2_routes_test`) already cover the
API contract; these entries check it on a real engine and with real recordings.

### W2-08 Meetings API v2: calendar binding, summary endpoints, room-change route

- [ ] **SMK-W2-08-1** (Runtime) Harness: calendar binding, summary save and room change on a running engine
  - Setup: release-dev engine on `$FP_PORT` with a fresh `FP_DD`; no meeting app open.
  - Steps: 1. `api /meetings/start -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"title":"Weekly sync","calendar_event_id":"evt1"}'` and note the `id` (A). 2. `api /meetings/stop -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"id":A}'`. 3. `api /meetings/start -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"title":"Hallway chat"}'` and note the `id` (B). 4. `api /meetings/B -X PUT -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"title":"Weekly sync","calendar_event_id":"evt1"}'`. 5. `api /meetings/B/summary -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"summary":"x"}'`, then the same with `{"summary":"  "}`. 6. `api /meetings/B/summary-status`. 7. `api /meetings/room-change -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"meetingId":B,"token":"t1","decision":"switch"}'`. 8. `sqlq "select id, meeting_end, end_reason, detection_source, calendar_event_id from meetings order by id"`.
  - Expected: step 1 binds (`calendar_event_id` of A is `evt1` in step 8); step 4 answers 200 and B keeps the title "Hallway chat"; step 5 returns the meeting with a note ending in `## Summary\nx`, and the blank summary answers 400; step 6 answers `"state":"idle"`; step 7 returns `newMeetingId` C; in step 8 B has `end_reason` `room_changed` and C is open with `detection_source` `audio_process_room_change`. `api /meetings/status` shows C as the active meeting.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-08-2** (Runtime) Live transcription follows a room change
  - Setup: release-dev engine with audio capture and live meeting transcription on (your own Deepgram key, or the selected local engine); a call or video with speech playing.
  - Steps: 1. Start a meeting with `api /meetings/start -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"app":"smoke"}'` and let speech play for a minute. 2. Switch it with `api /meetings/room-change -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"meetingId":<id>,"token":"t1","decision":"switch"}'`. 3. Let speech play for another minute, then stop the new meeting with `api /meetings/stop -X POST`. 4. `api /meetings/<old id>/transcript` and `api /meetings/<new id>/transcript`. 5. `grep -E "room change|meeting streaming" "$FP_DD/engine.log"`.
  - Expected: speech before the switch is in the old meeting's transcript and speech after it in the new one's; neither transcript is empty and no line appears in both. The log shows the switch and a streaming session starting for the new meeting.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-08-3** (Runtime) Export without audio writes a silent video
  - Setup: release-dev engine with screen and audio capture on and ffmpeg available; a meeting of at least two minutes that has ended (start and stop one with `api /meetings/start` and `api /meetings/stop` while something plays).
  - Steps: 1. `api /export -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"meeting_id":<id>,"include_audio":false}'`. 2. `api /export -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"meeting_id":<id>}'`. 3. Open both files from `$FP_DD/exports/`, or run `ffprobe -v error -show_streams <file>` on each.
  - Expected: step 1 answers with `audio_chunk_count` 0 and an mp4 with a video stream and no audio stream; step 2 has a non-zero `audio_chunk_count` and an audio stream.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-08-4** (UI smoke) The bundled meeting-summary pipe saves a summary with your own AI preset
  - Setup: dev app (`scripts/dev/app-dev.sh`) on the isolated data dir with a default AI preset of your own (for example a local Ollama model or your own API key) and no Freenpipe or screenpipe cloud login; the Meeting Summary pipe enabled; audio capture on.
  - Steps: 1. Start a meeting from the app, write a line of your own in its note, and talk or play a call for two minutes. 2. Stop the meeting. 3. Wait for the pipe run to finish (Pipes page, or `api /pipes/meeting-summary/executions`). 4. Reopen the meeting note.
  - Expected: the run uses your own preset's model and sends nothing to any screenpipe or Freenpipe host; its output shows the section starting with `## Summary` and a `POST /meetings/<id>/summary` call; the note keeps your line and gains one `## Summary` section; an untitled meeting gets a short title.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-08-5** (UI smoke) "Summarize with AI" in the meeting note saves through the summary endpoint
  - Setup: dev app as in SMK-W2-08-4; an ended meeting with a transcript and a note containing a heading of your own (for example `## Agenda`).
  - Steps: 1. Open the meeting note and press the summarize button. 2. Let the chat finish. 3. Reopen the note. 4. Press the button again and reopen the note once more.
  - Expected: the chat calls `POST /meetings/<id>/summary` (not PUT or PATCH); the note keeps `## Agenda` and its text and has exactly one `## Summary` section after both runs.
