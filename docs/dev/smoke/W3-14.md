# W3-14 Windows smoke checks

Entries follow the format in [../windows-smoke-checklist.md](../windows-smoke-checklist.md). Run
them on a W3-14 (or later) build in the isolated dev setup: `FP_DD`, port 3035,
`SCREENPIPE_API_KEY`. `api` and `sqlq` are the helpers from PLAN.md section 4. Start the engine with
the mode under test, for example
`target/release-dev/screenpipe.exe record --port $FP_PORT --data-dir "$FP_DD" --audio-capture-mode meetings-only > "$FP_DD/engine.log" 2>&1 &`,
and stop it (`taskkill //IM screenpipe.exe //F` for the dev build only) before switching modes.

### W3-14 Audio capture modes and device status events

- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-1** (Runtime) `disabled` mode never starts audio and the audio routes refuse
  - Setup: engine started with `--audio-capture-mode disabled` on a fresh `$FP_DD`; a microphone and speakers are connected.
  - Steps: 1. Wait 2 minutes. 2. `api /audio/start -X POST -i`. 3. `api /audio/device/start -X POST -i -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"device_name":"<any name from api /audio/list>"}'`. 4. `api /health` and read `audio_capture_mode`, `audio_status`, `capture_status.status`. 5. `sqlq "select count(*) from audio_chunks"` and `ls "$FP_DD/data"`. 6. `grep -i "audio capture is disabled\|meeting detector disabled" "$FP_DD/engine.log"`.
  - Expected: steps 2 and 3 answer `409 Conflict` with exactly `{"success":false,"message":"Audio capture is disabled in settings"}`. `/health` has `audio_capture_mode` `disabled`, `audio_status` `disabled`, `capture_status.status` `disabled` and `status` `healthy`. The count is 0 and no `.mp4`/`.wav` audio files exist. The log says the meeting detector is disabled because audio capture is disabled. The Windows privacy indicator never shows the microphone in use.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-2** (Hardware) `meetings-only` records only between meeting start and stop
  - Setup: engine started with `--audio-capture-mode meetings-only` on a fresh `$FP_DD`; microphone and speakers connected; no meeting app open.
  - Steps: 1. Wait 2 minutes, then `sqlq "select count(*) from audio_chunks"` and `api /audio/device/status`. 2. `api /health` and read `audio_status`, `capture_status.status`, `status`. 3. `api /meetings/start -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{}'` and note the time. 4. Speak and play a video with sound for 90 seconds. 5. `api /audio/device/status`. 6. `api /meetings/stop -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{}'` and note the time. 7. Wait 2 minutes, then `api /audio/device/status` and `sqlq "select id, timestamp from audio_chunks order by id"`. 8. `grep "meetings-only" "$FP_DD/engine.log"`.
  - Expected: step 1 count is 0 and every device has `is_running` false; step 2 shows `audio_status` `ok`, `capture_status.status` `waiting_for_meeting` and `status` `healthy` (no audio stall). Within a few seconds of step 3 the configured microphone and output are `is_running` true (step 5). Step 7: chunks exist only with timestamps between the start time and roughly 30 s after the stop time, the devices are `is_running` false again, and no new chunk appears afterwards. The log shows the meeting being confirmed, `dropping audio chunk ... no meeting was confirmed` only for audio recorded after the stop, and `released N audio stream(s) while waiting for a meeting` about 30 s after the stop. The microphone privacy indicator is off before step 3 and again after step 7.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-3** (Hardware) `meetings-only` follows a detected Teams or Zoom call
  - Setup: as SMK-W3-14-2, meeting detection enabled (default); Teams or Zoom installed.
  - Steps: 1. Join a test call (Teams "Meet now" or a Zoom test meeting) and talk for 2 minutes. 2. Leave the call and wait 2 minutes. 3. `sqlq "select id, timestamp from audio_chunks order by id"` and `api /meetings?limit=1`.
  - Expected: devices open within about 30 s of the call being detected (the detector scans less often while no audio is open), chunks cover the call, and the streams close about 30 s after the meeting row gets its end time. No chunk is recorded while the call is not running.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-4** (Runtime) Device stop and start publish `audio_device_status_changed` on `/ws/events`
  - Setup: engine started with the default mode (`always`) and a microphone recording; `websocat` (or any websocket client) available.
  - Steps: 1. `websocat "ws://127.0.0.1:$FP_PORT/ws/events?token=$SCREENPIPE_API_KEY" | grep --line-buffered audio_device_status_changed` in a second terminal. 2. `api /audio/device/stop -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"device_name":"<microphone name from /audio/device/status>"}'`. 3. Start it again with `/audio/device/start` and the same body.
  - Expected: step 2 prints one event with `device_name` set to the microphone, `is_running` false, `is_user_disabled` true and `reason` `user_paused`; step 3 prints `is_running` true, `is_user_disabled` false (reason `started`). The device list on the app's home page updates at once when the app is running.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-5** (UI smoke) Microphone loss notifications are shown once and respect the preferences
  - Setup: dev app (`scripts/dev/app-dev.sh`) with a USB or Bluetooth microphone selected explicitly in recording settings ("follow system default" off) and the built-in microphone not paused.
  - Steps: 1. Unplug (or switch off) the selected microphone and wait 30 s. 2. Plug it back in and wait 10 s. 3. Repeat steps 1-2 twice within 5 minutes. 4. Pause the built-in microphone from the home page, unplug the selected microphone and wait 30 s. 5. Turn off "capture stalls" in notification settings and repeat step 1.
  - Expected: step 1 shows one operating-system notification that the microphone switched to the built-in one; step 2 shows one "Microphone restored" notification. The repeats in step 3 show no further notifications (throttled). Step 4 shows "No microphone available" naming the unplugged device. Step 5 shows nothing. The app log has the kebab-case events `audio-device-fallback-engaged`, `-cleared` and `-unavailable` each time.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-6** (UI smoke) Tray shows the waiting state in `meetings-only` mode
  - Setup: dev app with `audioCaptureMode` set to `meetings-only` in the settings store (edit `store.bin` settings or use the settings UI once W5-20 adds the selector), then "apply & restart".
  - Steps: 1. Open the tray menu with no meeting running. 2. Start a meeting from the app (or `api /meetings/start`), wait 10 s and open the tray menu again. 3. Turn meeting detection off in settings, restart, open the tray menu.
  - Expected: step 1 reads "● Recording screen · audio starts with a meeting"; step 2 reads "● Recording"; step 3 reads "● Recording screen · audio needs meeting detection". Saving a change to `audioCaptureMode` or `disableAudio` restarts the whole server (toast "Freenpipe server restarted with new settings").
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W3-14-7** (Hardware) `meetings-only` keeps working across several meetings and a restart
  - Setup: as SMK-W3-14-2 (engine with `--audio-capture-mode meetings-only`, fresh `$FP_DD`, microphone and speakers connected). Meeting detection off for step 6 (`--disable-meeting-detector` or the settings toggle).
  - Steps: 1. `api /meetings/start -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{}'`, speak for 60 s, `api /audio/device/status`, then `api /meetings/stop -X POST -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{}'`. 2. Wait 60 s and `api /health`. 3. Repeat step 1 (a second meeting). 4. `grep -c "opening .* configured audio device" "$FP_DD/engine.log"` and `grep -i "stopping all devices\|device monitor" "$FP_DD/engine.log" | tail`. 5. Start a meeting, kill the engine with `taskkill //IM screenpipe.exe //F` (dev build only) while it runs, and start the engine again with meeting detection on; wait 30 s and `api /audio/device/status`. 6. Repeat step 5 with meeting detection off, but before restarting set the open row's start 13 hours back: `sqlq "update meetings set meeting_start = strftime('%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%fZ','now','-13 hours') where meeting_end is null"`.
  - Expected: both meetings open the microphone and output within a few seconds (`is_running` true during each), and both close about 30 s after their stop. Between the meetings `/health` shows `audio_status` `ok` and `capture_status.status` `waiting_for_meeting`, never a stalled audio warning. The log shows the devices being opened twice and no teardown of the whole pipeline in between. In step 5 the manual meeting left open by the kill is adopted (devices `is_running` true, log "meeting N is already in progress"). In step 6 it is not adopted (log "not adopting open meeting"), and no device opens until a new meeting starts.
