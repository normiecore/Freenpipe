# W2-17 Windows smoke checks

Entries follow the format in [../windows-smoke-checklist.md](../windows-smoke-checklist.md). Run
them on a W2-17 (or later) build in the isolated dev setup: `FP_DD`, port 3035,
`SCREENPIPE_API_KEY`. `api` and `sqlq` are the helpers from PLAN.md section 4. The live meeting
stream needs your own Deepgram key (Settings, or `SCREENPIPE_MEETING_DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`); without
one, use the selected local engine and skip the checks that need speaker labels.

### W2-17 Stream-scoped live speakers and strict meeting-segment mapping

- [ ] **SMK-W2-17-1** (Runtime) Live finals are stored with their stream scope
  - Setup: release-dev engine on `$FP_PORT` with a fresh `FP_DD`, live meeting transcription on with a Deepgram key; a call or video with at least two other voices playing through the speakers.
  - Steps: 1. `api /meetings/start -X POST -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"app":"smoke"}'` (or join a detected call). 2. Talk into the microphone and let the call play for two minutes. 3. `sqlq "select id, device_type, stream_id, session_speaker_id, speaker_name, item_id from meeting_transcript_segments order by id desc limit 20"`. 4. `api /meetings/<id>/transcript`.
  - Expected: every new row has a `stream_id` other than `legacy` (one per device), `item_id` values of the form `deepgram:<stream_id>:turn:...`, and output rows with `session_speaker_id` values like `deepgram:<stream_id>:speaker:1`; step 4 returns live rows with a `sessionSpeakerId` field.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-17-2** (Runtime) A provider reconnect starts a new speaker scope
  - Setup: the meeting from SMK-W2-17-1 still running.
  - Steps: 1. Disconnect the network for about 20 s, then reconnect. 2. Keep the call playing for another minute. 3. `grep "starting Deepgram live stream" "$FP_DD/engine.log"`. 4. Repeat the `sqlq` from SMK-W2-17-1 step 3.
  - Expected: the log shows a second stream start for the same device with a different `stream_id`; rows after the reconnect carry the new `stream_id`, and a "speaker 1" after the reconnect has a different `session_speaker_id` from the "speaker 1" before it; no transcript lines after the reconnect are missing.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-17-3** (Runtime) Timeline rows of a live meeting are flagged and keep their own ids
  - Setup: the meeting from SMK-W2-17-1, stopped with `api /meetings/stop -X POST`; screen capture on so the meeting window has frames. A WebSocket client (`websocat`) or the dev app's devtools.
  - Steps: 1. `websocat "ws://127.0.0.1:$FP_PORT/stream/frames?token=$SCREENPIPE_API_KEY"` and send `{"start_time":"<meeting start, RFC 3339>","end_time":"<meeting end>","order":"descending"}` (a past window, so the database path answers). 2. Inspect the `audio` arrays of the returned frames. 3. Alternatively open the timeline in the dev app at the meeting time and read the `/stream/frames` WebSocket frames in the devtools Network tab.
  - Expected: audio entries that came from the live meeting have `"is_live": true` and negative `audio_chunk_id` values that differ from each other (one per segment); background entries have `"is_live": false` and positive chunk ids; no live line is missing or duplicated on the timeline.
  - Result:
- [ ] **SMK-W2-17-4** (Runtime) Speaker backfill after a meeting labels only clean chunks
  - Setup: engine from SMK-W2-17-1 with the speaker models downloaded; a meeting with two remote voices that ended at least five minutes ago.
  - Steps: 1. Wait for a reconciliation sweep (`grep "mapped .* live meeting segment" "$FP_DD/engine.log"`). 2. `sqlq "select m.id, m.session_speaker_id, m.speaker_id, a.speaker_id from meeting_transcript_segments m join audio_transcriptions a on a.transcription = m.transcript and a.transcription_engine = 'live' and julianday(a.timestamp) = julianday(m.captured_at) order by m.id desc limit 30"`.
  - Expected: wherever a segment has a `speaker_id`, it equals its mirror's `speaker_id`; segments whose chunk held both remote voices stay without a `speaker_id`, and the meeting transcript keeps showing their "speaker N" label.
